Questions you may have

Good to know.

Do I have to talk in the sessions?

Not at all. Many people simply listen at first. You can share when — and if — you feel ready.

Will my personal details be kept private?

Yes. Your name, number and anything you share in the form are kept 100% confidential and are used only to support you and place you in a suitable group. A nickname is fine if you prefer.

What if the group isn't right for me?

That's completely okay. Attend the first session, and if it doesn't feel right, your ₹199 will be fully refunded before the 2nd session.

How will I be added to the group?

After you submit the form and we verify your payment, we'll reach out to you on WhatsApp with the joining details.

Is this the same as therapy?

A support group is a guided, shared space to talk, listen and learn coping skills together. It doesn't replace individual therapy or medical treatment, but it can be a meaningful source of support alongside them.

I'd like to help others. Can I volunteer?

We'd love that! There's a question in the form where you can tell us if you'd like to help host sessions.
